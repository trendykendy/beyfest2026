# The Pugilist: likeness sheet

Player: Shane. From 3 reference photos (in the git-ignored
`reference-photos/pugilist/`), 2026-10-30. Upload copies, cropped so only he is in
them, are in `reference-photos/pugilist/upload/`.

**Master reference: the balcony photo** (drinking from a bottle, light blue T-shirt;
the user's pick, 2026-10-30), to be saved as `upload/2-photo-master-front.jpg`. It
was taken at the same time as the restaurant photo (round glasses), but the user
says that one is a bad angle: **his face isn't that full.** Use the restaurant photo
only for his glasses, and the two older photos for the eyes, brows and beard line.

**The Pugilist is also the STYLE reference for the whole set.** The original is kept
as `approved/pugilist-original.png`, and the style uploads are copies of it, so
changing his art does not change the reference.

## Anchors (carry these into every image)

- **Hair:** dark brown, short and neat, a little longer and pushed up/back on top,
  short at the sides. Not big or spiky.
- **Face shape:** a medium oval with a broad forehead and a defined jaw under the
  beard. Not round or chubby, and not long and chiselled either. Younger-looking
  than the current art.
- **Facial hair:** a full, dark brown beard, short-to-medium, neatly shaped along the
  cheek line, fuller at the chin, joined to a moustache.
- **Eyes:** dark brown, fairly deep-set, slightly heavy-lidded; thick, straight dark
  brows.
- **Glasses:** in real life, round tortoiseshell-and-gold frames. **The art keeps its
  purple wraparound shades** (user's call, 2026-10-30).
- **Nose:** straight, medium, slightly broad at the tip.
- **Mouth:** full lower lip; a dry, deadpan look is typical (the restaurant photo).
- **Skin:** fair.
- **Build:** medium and solid, with ordinary arms. Not a shredded, bulky fighter.

## Where the current art has drifted

The current `pugilist.png` has:
- a long, chiselled, square-jawed hero face, where his is a medium oval, younger and softer
- big, swept-up, purple-tinted hair, where his is short and neat
- an older-looking, rugged face

## Exaggerate slightly

The beard and the thick straight brows.
