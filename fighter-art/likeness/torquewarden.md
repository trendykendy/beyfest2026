# The Torque Warden: likeness sheet

Player: Moss. From 4 reference photos (in the git-ignored `reference-photos/torquewarden/`),
2026-10-28. Upload copies, cropped so only he is in them, are in
`reference-photos/torquewarden/upload/` (front, and a low-angle shot of the beard).
The other two (one tiny, one in a bar with other people and green lighting) aren't used.

## Anchors (carry these into every image)

- **Head:** fully bald and smooth on top, with a large, rounded dome. Under the fedora, the hat sits on a bald head: no hair shows at the sides or back.
- **Face shape:** big and broad, with full, round cheeks. Wide, not lean or angular.
- **Beard:** a big, long, full beard, dark brown with a reddish-auburn tint, thick and slightly wiry, reaching well below the chin to the chest. A full moustache blending into it.
- **Eyes:** smallish and deep-set, light grey-blue, under fairly heavy brows.
- **Brows:** dark brown, straight and heavy.
- **Nose:** broad and straight, with a rounded tip.
- **Skin:** fair, ruddy at the cheeks.
- **Ears:** stand out a little from the bald head.
- **Build:** big and heavy-set, broad shoulders and chest, thick neck. Large, not a bodybuilder.
- **Glasses:** none.

## Where the current art has drifted

The current `torquewarden.png` (made in Gemini) has:
- a lean, angular face and a short, squared-off beard, where his is a big round face with a long, full beard down to the chest
- a medium build, where he is big and heavy-set
- no sign that he's bald under the hat

The long, full beard, the broad round face, and the heavy brows over a fierce glare.
