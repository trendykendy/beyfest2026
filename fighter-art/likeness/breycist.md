# The Breycist: likeness sheet

Player: Conor. From 4 reference photos (in the git-ignored `reference-photos/breycist/`),
2026-10-27. Upload copies, cropped so only he is in them, are in `reference-photos/breycist/upload/`.

## Anchors (carry these into every image)

- **Face shape:** broad, rounded-square face with full cheeks and a soft, rounded jaw. Not a sharp V-shaped anime chin.
- **Hair:** short brown, cut close at the sides. A little longer and textured on top, standing up in short spikes at the front. Straight hairline, slightly higher at the temples.
- **Eyes:** smallish and narrow-set under the brows, blue-grey, crinkling almost shut when he smiles.
- **Brows:** light brown, fairly straight, sitting low.
- **Nose:** straight and medium length, with a soft, rounded tip.
- **Mouth:** a wide smile that shows the upper teeth.
- **Skin:** fair, with ruddy pink cheeks.
- **Facial hair:** none; clean-shaven.
- **Ears:** stand out slightly.
- **Build:** solid and stocky, with broad shoulders and a thick neck. Not lean or wiry.
- **Glasses:** worn in 2 of the 4 photos (thin rectangular frames). The character has none for now; this is the user's call.

The current `breycist.png` has:

- large, wide anime eyes, where his are smaller and narrower
- tanned skin, where his is fair and ruddy
