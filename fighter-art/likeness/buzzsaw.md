# The Buzzsaw: likeness sheet

Player: Sean. From 5 reference photos (in the git-ignored `reference-photos/buzzsaw/`),
2026-10-28. Upload copies, cropped so only he is in them, are in
`reference-photos/buzzsaw/upload/` (front, close-up, head and shoulders). The other
two (one with a child in frame, one in a cap and a mirror) aren't used.

The user chose the photos over the current art for his likeness.

## Anchors (carry these into every image)

- **Head:** fully bald and smooth, a high, rounded dome.
- **Glasses:** browline glasses: a thick black upper rim with thin silver wire lower rims and silver temples. His signature. (The current art has plain black rectangles.)
- **Beard:** a short, neatly trimmed full beard, dark brown with grey at the chin and sides, and a matching moustache.
- **Face shape:** fairly long, with a high forehead and a defined but not sharp jaw; lean in the cheeks.
- **Eyes:** blue-grey, under fairly straight dark brows.
- **Nose:** long and straight.
- **Mouth:** a closed-mouth smile or a slight smirk.
- **Ears:** stand out a little from the bald head.
- **Skin:** fair.
- **Build:** slim to average, not muscular.

## Where the current art has drifted

The current `buzzsaw.png` has:
- plain black rectangular glasses, where his are browline glasses with silver lower rims
- an all-brown beard, where his has grey at the chin
- big, muscular arms and a heavy build, where he is slim to average
- the fist-at-camera lunge, which only the Pugilist keeps
- a dark glow baked in behind him instead of a clean cut-out

## Exaggerate slightly

The browline glasses, the shine on the bald head, and the grey streaks in the beard.
