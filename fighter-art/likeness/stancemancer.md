# The Stancemancer: likeness sheet

Player: McHale. From 3 reference photos (in the git-ignored `reference-photos/stancemancer/`),
2026-10-28. Upload copies are in `reference-photos/stancemancer/upload/` (front smile,
front neutral; both show only him, so they aren't cropped). The third photo is black
and white, with people behind him, so it isn't used.

## Anchors (carry these into every image)

- **Glasses:** dark, thick-rimmed rectangular glasses (dark brown or black frames). His signature, as the bio says: "Don't let the glasses fool you."
- **Hair:** medium-short, reddish-brown with a ginger tint, a bit untidy, swept up and to the side at the front, with a few strands sticking up. Not blond.
- **Face shape:** long, fairly narrow face with a high forehead and a defined jaw. Lean, not round.
- **Stubble:** light ginger stubble on the jaw and upper lip. Not a full beard.
- **Eyes:** blue-grey, behind the glasses.
- **Brows:** fairly thin, light brown.
- **Nose:** long and straight.
- **Mouth:** thin lips; a small, closed-mouth half-smile.
- **Skin:** fair, slightly ruddy.
- **Build:** slim, average height.

## Where the current art has drifted

The current `stancemancer.png` has:
- bright blond hair, where his is reddish-brown and ginger
- no stubble
- a heroic, broad-shouldered build, where he is slim
- the fist-at-camera lunge, which only the Pugilist keeps

## Exaggerate slightly

The thick-rimmed glasses, the untidy swept-up hair, and the long face.
