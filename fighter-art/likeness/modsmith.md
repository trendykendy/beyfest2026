# The Modsmith: likeness sheet

Player: Cuffe. From 4 reference photos (in the git-ignored `reference-photos/modsmith/`),
2026-10-28. Upload copies, cropped so only he is in them, are in
`reference-photos/modsmith/upload/` (three-quarter, front, close-up). The fourth
(a wide-angle shot looking up, with a warped face) isn't used.

The user chose the photos over the current art for his likeness.

## Anchors (carry these into every image)

- **Hair:** dark brown, medium-short, thick and tousled, swept up and back at the front with a messy, slightly wild top; shorter at the sides.
- **Face shape:** long and fairly lean, with high cheekbones, a long straight nose and a narrow, pointed chin. Not broad or square.
- **Facial hair:** a thin moustache and a short chinstrap beard along the jaw, fuller at the chin like a goatee, dark brown. Neatly kept, not a full heavy beard.
- **Eyes:** deep-set, dark brown to hazel, under heavy, straight dark brows; often a sceptical, narrowed look.
- **Mouth:** thin lips; a dry half-smile, or wide open when he laughs.
- **Skin:** fair.
- **Build:** slim and wiry. Not muscular.

## Where the current art has drifted

The current `modsmith.png` has:
- a broad, square-jawed, heroic face, where his is long and lean with a pointed chin
- a heavy, full goatee, where his is a thinner moustache and chinstrap
- a muscular build, where he is slim
- the fist-at-camera lunge, which only the Pugilist keeps
- a speckled, noisy background instead of a clean cut-out

## Exaggerate slightly

The tousled, wild hair, the long face and nose, and the narrowed, calculating eyes.
