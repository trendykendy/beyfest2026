# The Joyzerker: likeness sheet

Player: Vhar. From 5 reference photos (in the git-ignored `reference-photos/joyzerker/`),
2026-10-28. Upload copies, cropped so only he is in them, are in
`reference-photos/joyzerker/upload/` (front smile, three-quarter laugh, front neutral).

## Anchors (carry these into every image)

- **Face shape:** broad and full, with round, full cheeks and a high forehead. A big, friendly face, not lean or angular.
- **Hair:** medium-length brown hair with an auburn tint, swept back off the forehead in loose waves, reaching the ears and collar at the back. A few loose strands fall forward at the front. Slightly higher at the temples. Not short, not spiky, not a ponytail.
- **Beard:** a big, full ginger-red beard, redder than his hair, long and rounded at the chin. A full moustache whose ends can curl up a little.
- **Eyes:** smallish, blue-grey, crinkling almost shut when he grins.
- **Brows:** light brown, fairly straight.
- **Nose:** straight, medium-broad, with a rounded tip.
- **Mouth:** a big, toothy grin that shows his upper teeth through the beard.
- **Skin:** fair, with ruddy pink cheeks.
- **Build:** large and heavy-set, broad shoulders and chest. Big, not a bodybuilder.
- **Glasses:** none (sunglasses in one photo only).

## Where the current art has drifted

The current `joyzerker.png` (made in Gemini) has:
- dark brown hair and beard, where his beard is ginger-red and his hair brown-auburn
- a lean, angular face, where his is broad and round
- a bodybuilder physique with bulging veins, where he is heavy-set
- purple face paint and split-colour skin, which the style guide rules out
- a gritted-teeth rage face, where his natural look is a huge grin

## Exaggerate slightly

The big ginger beard, the round cheeks, and the huge grin.
