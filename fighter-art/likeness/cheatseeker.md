Player: Tadhg. From 4 reference photos (in the git-ignored `reference-photos/cheatseeker/`),

## Anchors (carry these into every image)

- **Hair:** short dark brown hair with a reddish tint in some light, neat and fairly flat, swept back and slightly to the side at the front. Short at the sides. Not spiky.
- **Face shape:** broad, full face with round cheeks and a soft jaw. Not angular.
- **Facial hair:** short ginger-red stubble or a short beard along the jaw and chin, redder than his hair.
- **Eyes:** smallish and narrow, crinkling almost shut when he grins.
- **Brows:** dark brown, fairly straight and heavy.
- **Nose:** straight and broad, with a rounded tip.
- **Mouth:** a wide, toothy grin.
- **Skin:** fair, ruddy at the cheeks.
- **Build:** solid, stocky, broad shoulders. Not a lean, heroic build.
