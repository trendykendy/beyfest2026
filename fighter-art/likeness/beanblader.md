# The Beanblader: likeness sheet

Player: Ronan. From 3 reference photos (in the git-ignored
`reference-photos/beanblader/`), 2026-10-30. Upload copies, cropped so only he is in
them, are in `reference-photos/beanblader/upload/`.

**The master reference is the most recent photo** (`2-photo-master-front.jpg`, the
user's call). The other two are older (longer, wavier hair, a fuller and redder
beard): use them for face shape and smile only. Where they disagree, the master wins.

## Anchors (carry these into every image)

- **Hair:** dark brown, short and neat, a little longer and swept slightly up at the
  front; the hairline recedes a touch at the temples, giving a high forehead. Not
  long, not curly, not spiky.
- **Face shape:** long and narrow, with a high forehead, slightly hollow cheeks and a
  long jaw. Not broad, square or heroic.
- **Facial hair:** a short, neatly trimmed ginger-auburn beard and moustache, close
  along the jaw, with grey flecks at the chin. Clearly redder than his hair.
- **Eyes:** pale blue-grey, slightly hooded and crinkling at the corners when he
  smiles; straight, fairly light brows.
- **Nose:** long and straight.
- **Ears:** noticeable, sticking out a little.
- **Mouth:** a wide, open, toothy grin.
- **Skin:** fair, ruddy cheeks.
- **Build:** slim, lean, tall. Not muscular or bulky.

## Where the current art has drifted

The current `beanblader.png` has:
- a broad, square-jawed, muscular hero, where Ronan is long-faced, lean and slim
- a full dark brown beard, where his is short and ginger-auburn
- tousled, spiky hair and bright saturated blue eyes
- an open-hand reach toward the Beyblade with no launcher or ripcord (breaks the
  hands rule), close to the Pugilist's lunge

## Exaggerate slightly

The long face and high forehead, the ginger beard against the dark hair, the ears,
and the big crinkly-eyed grin.
