## Why the photos weren't used in the end

The user: the original action image "is a really good likeness, I wouldn't stray too
far from it". For a fighter whose existing art already looks like the person, use
that art as the likeness reference instead of photos. The photos stay on file. The
likeness sheet (`likeness/stancemancer.md`) describes the photos, so it's out of
date for him; the original art wins.

## What worked

  OTHER leg extended out STRAIGHT to the side, fully straight at the knee".
