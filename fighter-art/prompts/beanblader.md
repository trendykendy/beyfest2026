## Prompt A

> Attached: the first two images are the STYLE REFERENCE ONLY (a fighter called the
> Pugilist, full figure and a head close-up): copy their hand-drawn, inked rendering
> exactly, but none of his face, outfit, colours or pose. The three photos are of
> the real man this character is based on, for LIKENESS ONLY. The photo of him in a
> brown zip polo is the most recent and is the MASTER reference; the other two are
> older (his hair was longer then and his beard fuller): use them only for face
> shape and smile.
>
> [Reusable style block from STYLE-GUIDE.md, with PALETTE = crimson red, bronze and
> cream]
>
> Character: "The Beanblader", a calm warrior monk. Draw him in this hand-drawn
> inked style with his likeness: short, neat dark brown hair, slightly swept up at
> the front, the hairline receding a little at the temples (a high forehead); a
> long, narrow face with slightly hollow cheeks and a long jaw (not broad or
> square); a short, neatly trimmed ginger-auburn beard and moustache, clearly
> redder than his hair, with grey flecks at the chin; a long straight nose; ears
> that stick out a little; fair, ruddy skin; a slim, lean, tall build (not
> muscular).
>
> Pose: he levitates cross-legged in a lotus position, floating just above the
> ground, completely at ease, launching a Beyblade with no visible effort. One hand
> holds a plain Beyblade launcher out in front at chest height, the Beyblade just
> leaving it; the other hand draws the ripcord out in one slow, graceful arc. His
> large wooden prayer beads have come loose and float in a ring around him. The
> robe drifts upward as if weightless. It must NOT be a fist punched toward the
> camera. Camera: front, slightly low.
>
> Expression: serene but exaggerated, like an anime monk master: eyes closed in
> deep calm, crinkled at the corners, brows lifted gently, a broad, peaceful,
> closed-mouth smile. Not a realistic face.
>
> Outfit, a light cyberpunk lean on a monk's robe (same silhouette): a crimson
> tech-monk robe with panel seams and thin glowing bronze trim, over a cream
> undershirt; dark wrapped trousers; the large wooden beads carry faint glowing
> bronze runes (no letters). Beyblade: bronze and cream, wood-grain textures,
> glowing crimson energy.
