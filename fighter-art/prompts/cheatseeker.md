## What worked

- **Quizzical:** "one eyebrow raised high and arched, the other pulled down low in a
  suspicious squint; eyes narrowed… head tilted slightly; mouth closed in a skeptical,
  lopsided half-smirk… as if saying hmm, really?"
- **Pulling back from chiselled:** "softer and fuller: rounder, fuller cheeks, a soft,
  rounded jaw and chin (no sharp jawline, no prominent cheekbones)… a friendly,
  boyish face, not a rugged action hero." Editing toward the poster had made him
  more angular, so the face had to be brought back toward the photos.
- **RULE BOOK:** asking for "clear gold capital letters" kept the lettering readable
  through all four versions.

## Victory images (2026-10-28): approved

The same recipe as the others (scene template + approved cut-out + Pugilist head
close-up, named by what each shows). Both came out right first time.

- **Belt:** the template already had a RULE BOOK in his other hand; the prompt asked
  for it to match the new book (navy, gold corners, gold capitals, crown crest).
- **Royale:** both hands are on the trophy, so the prompt asked for the book tucked
  under an arm or in the jacket; it came out in the inside pocket, still readable.
  The arms stayed a bit muscular from the template.
- Victory images are never flipped, so RULE BOOK needs no mirror image there.
