## What worked

- **Quizzical:** "one eyebrow raised high and arched, the other pulled down low in a
  suspicious squint; eyes narrowed… head tilted slightly; mouth closed in a skeptical,
  lopsided half-smirk… as if saying hmm, really?"
- **Pulling back from chiselled:** "softer and fuller: rounder, fuller cheeks, a soft,
  rounded jaw and chin (no sharp jawline, no prominent cheekbones)… a friendly,
  boyish face, not a rugged action hero." Editing toward the poster had made him
  more angular, so the face had to be brought back toward the photos.
- **RULE BOOK:** asking for "clear gold capital letters" kept the lettering readable
  through all four versions.
