## What worked

- **A modded Beyblade as the star:** "its body covered in visible circuit-board traces,
  tiny blinking LED lights in orange and blue, small exposed wires, screws, tiny vents,
  a little antenna and other small mechanical greebles bolted on".
- **A plainer launcher:** "a normal, recognisable Beyblade launcher with just one or
  two small custom touches… no rifle scope, no big gun-like body".
- **Ripcord free:** "FULLY pulled out of the launcher… held only in his raised hand…
  its loose end flying in the air… No line between his hand and the launcher."
- To edit one of two results: answer ChatGPT's "which image do you like more?" with
  that image, then confirm by byte size before sending the edit.
