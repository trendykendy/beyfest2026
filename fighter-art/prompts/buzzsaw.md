## The character

The user: the Buzzsaw's whole thing is that **his launches are stylish and absurd**.
The plan's discus twist was too ordinary. Options offered: a behind-the-back
leap, a mid-backflip launch, a one-leg pirouette, a between-the-legs trick shot.
The user picked the backflip.

## What worked

- **Pose:** "UPSIDE DOWN at the very top of a backflip, fully inverted in mid-air,
  body arched, legs tucked up over him, and at that moment he fires the Beyblade
  STRAIGHT DOWN… Totally unnecessary, totally confident."
- **Cyber glasses:** "the same browline shape, but with a sleek black upper rim with
  a thin glowing blue line along it, thin silver lower rims, and lenses with a faint
  blue HUD tint".
- **No extra ripcord strands:** "exactly one launcher and one plain ripcord that ends
  in his fingers (no extra strands, no handle, no ring)".

## Known side effect

His face is upside down, so screens that crop around the face point (royale draw
wheel, qualified screen, phone card) show it inverted. The user approved it anyway.
