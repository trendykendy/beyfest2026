# Beyfest fighter art: pose plan

One unique action pose per starter fighter, for the action cut-outs. Planned
together so no two match. Style rules are in [STYLE-GUIDE.md](STYLE-GUIDE.md).

Only the Stancemancer and the Joyzerker have written bios (on the public site).
The other poses are drawn from the fighter's name and current outfit; anything
marked *(assumed)* is a guess to confirm.

## Spread across the set

| Silhouette | Fighters |
|---|---|
| Low to the ground | Beanblader (slide), Underdogger (rising from a knee) |
| In the air | Beyby (leap) |
| Twisting or spinning | Buzzsaw (discus spin), Fairy Dancer (pirouette) |
| Still and planted | Stancemancer (wide stance), Torque Warden (immovable), Cheatseeker (rule book raised) |
| Aiming | Modsmith (sight down the launcher) |
| Punch at camera | Pugilist (the one keeper of the classic lunge) |
| Big and open | Joyzerker (arms thrown wide) |
| Walking away | Breycist (over the shoulder) |

Camera angles vary too: low, high, side and front, so the set doesn't read as
twelve versions of one shot.

## The poses

**1. The Beanblader** (Ronan): red jacket, beads, bronze and cream Beyblade
- **Pose:** a low sliding launch, one knee skimming the floor, the body leaning sideways into the slide, ripping the cord across the body. The jacket flares behind.
- **Camera:** low, side-on.
- **Mood:** playful and cocky *(assumed)*.

**2. The Beyby** (Eamon): hoodie, face mask, pink and purple
- **Pose:** leaping in mid-air, knees tucked, launching *downward* at the stadium below. The hood blows back.
- **Camera:** from below, looking up.
- **Mood:** small, fast, fearless *(assumed)*.

**3. The Breycist** (Conor): white Italian-style tracksuit, red and green
- **Pose:** walking away from a launch. The Beyblade spins behind him and he glances back over his shoulder with a smirk, launcher resting on his shoulder.
- **Camera:** three-quarter from behind, face turned toward the viewer.
- **Mood:** too cool to watch *(assumed)*.

**4. The Buzzsaw** (Sean): bald, glasses, beard, white T-shirt, jeans, blue
- **Pose:** a full-body twist like a discus thrower, mid-rotation, launching sideways. The Beyblade's trail curves around him like a saw blade.
- **Camera:** front, slightly high.
- **Mood:** gleeful power.
- **Note:** this replaces the current fist-at-camera lunge, which stays with the Pugilist.

**5. The Cheatseeker** (Tadhg): blue jacket, red top, rule book
- **Pose:** standing tall, rule book held up beside his face in one hand like a badge, launcher pointed down at his feet in the other. The Beyblade spins at his feet. A knowing squint.
- **Camera:** slightly low, front.
- **Mood:** "I've read the rules. Have you?"
- **Note:** the "RULE BOOK" lettering stays. It needs a hand-made mirror image (`scripts/unflip-text.py`), as before.

**6. The Fairy Dancer** (Steve): cap, pink and purple camo jacket
- **Pose:** mid-pirouette on one foot, the launch arm extended, the ripcord trailing in a spiral. The Beyblade's glow follows the spin like a ribbon.
- **Camera:** front, slightly low.
- **Mood:** graceful but deadly *(assumed)*.

**7. The Joyzerker** (Vhar): green and purple; *redesign, in the target style*
- **Bio:** "Joy on the outside. Zerker on the inside."
- **Pose:** leaning back after launching, arms thrown wide, head tipped back in a wild laugh-shout. Energy bursts outward: green on one side, purple on the other.
- **Camera:** low, front.
- **Mood:** the duality lives in the green/purple energy, **not** face paint or split-colour skin.

**8. The Modsmith** (Cuffe): white lab coat, blue shirt, orange and silver
- **Pose:** a crouch on one knee, the launcher raised to eye level and sighted down like a precision instrument, the other hand on the ripcord. Small glowing parts or tools float around the launcher.
- **Camera:** side-on, close.
- **Mood:** calculating; built this himself *(assumed)*.

**9. The Pugilist** (Shane): black and red gear, tinted glasses
- **Pose:** keeps the current punch-at-camera lunge, which suits a boxer. It's the one fighter that keeps this pose.
- **Camera:** front, wide-angle.
- **Mood:** joyful brawler.

**10. The Stancemancer** (McHale): glasses, blue jumper, purple
- **Bio:** "A master of posture and precision. His beyblade flows like water — but hits like a freight train. Don't let the glasses fool you." He "dropped into a wide stance" in the Frostbite Kid match.
- **Pose:** a deep, wide martial-arts stance, perfectly balanced, launcher held level at chest height, the free hand open and flat. The Beyblade's trail ripples like flowing water.
- **Camera:** front, square-on.
- **Mood:** calm, focused, unshakeable.

**11. The Torque Warden** (Moss): fedora, full beard, black coat, black and silver; *redesign, in the target style*
- **Pose:** standing completely still while everything moves around him. The coat billows, the launcher hangs at his side just after launching, and the Beyblade grinds at his feet throwing sparks. A stern stare from under the hat brim.
- **Camera:** low, front.
- **Mood:** the guardian who doesn't move *(assumed)*.
- **Note:** the "T.W" coat badge stays. It needs a hand-made mirror image, as before.

**12. The Underdogger** (Mark): long blond hair, waistcoat, bronze and orange
- **Pose:** pushing up off one knee mid-launch, off-balance but grinning, hair flying. A comeback in progress.
- **Camera:** low, three-quarter.
- **Mood:** never count him out.
