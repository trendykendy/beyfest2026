What is Beyfest?

Three Arenas.
One Champion.

Beyfest is back — and this time, the arena itself is your opponent. The 2026 edition introduces the Triple Threat format: three distinct battle arenas, randomly assigned before each bout. You don't just need to master your bey — you need to adapt on the fly.

Born from backyard beys and fueled by genuine competition, Beyfest has grown into the most hyped private Beyblade tournament in the community. Every arena throws different physics, different chaos, different stakes. Come prepared for all three.

The Battlegrounds

The Three Arenas

Before every bout, fate spins the wheel. One of these three stadiums is randomly selected — and the strategy you walked in with may suddenly mean nothing. Know your terrain. Know your bey. Pray for a draw that suits you.

I

The Neutral Ground

Xtreme Battle Beystadium

Standard build · No bias

The middle path. The honest fight. A balanced bowl that rewards skill above all else — no gimmicks, no mechanical interference, no terrain tricks. If your bey can't compete here, it can't compete anywhere. The arena where champions are forged by stamina.

Skill Balance Pure Combat
II

The Crusher

Double Xtreme Motorised

BX-00 · Mechanical platform

A living arena. The motorised central platform rises and falls without warning, crushing space between combatants and the acceleration rails, forcing a brutal close-quarters brawl. There's nowhere to run, no perimeter to circle. Pure attrition. The bey with strength and bone-density wins.

Stamina Strength Close Quarters
III

Death From Above

Drop Attack Beystadium

Elevated rail · Vertical assault

The arena that rewards aggression. Beys ride the upper rail and rain down on opponents from above, turning the fight three-dimensional. Hesitate and you'll be hit before you see it coming. Speed wins. Aggression wins. A clean line down the rail wins. Defenders need not apply.

Speed Aggression Vertical Game

Tournament Rules

How It Works

Beyfest 2026 is less rigid, more chaotic, and built for pure competition. The format is simple on paper but unpredictable in practice — first to 5 takes most matches, but everything else is shaped by the arena fate drops you into.

Between battles, swap parts. Change your bey. Adjust your approach. There are no locked builds — only adaptation. Every match begins the same way: a random arena, a clean launch, and no certainty beyond the next point.

01

Stadium System

All three Triple Threat stadiums are in active rotation. Before every match, one is randomly selected by draw — and that single stadium is used for the entire match.

  • No re-rolls. The first draw is the only draw.
  • No selection. You don't get a say in your battlefield.
  • No preference. The arena does not care who you are.
02

Equipment Rules

Builds are open. The toolbox is yours.

  • Any Beyblade parts may be used
  • Players may swap parts freely between battles
  • Multiple Beyblades may be used within a single match
  • All builds permitted — provided they're safe and functional
03

04

Battle Procedure

  • Stadium is randomly drawn
  • Players prepare beyblades
  • Launch call: "3, 2, 1, Let It Rip!"
  • Both players launch simultaneously
  • Result is judged — points awarded
  • Repeat until match point target is hit
05

Match Structure

A two-stage tournament. Group stage seeds your fate. Knockout stage tests it.

Group Stage

  • First to 5 points wins the match
  • Round-robin within groups
  • Standings by match wins
  • Tiebreakers by points difference

Knockout Qualification

  • Group winners advance to Winners Bracket
  • Everyone else falls into Losers Bracket
  • Double elimination overall
  • Two losses and you're out

06

Conduct & Fair Play

Beyfest runs on trust and competitive respect. Don't be the reason it doesn't.

  • No early or delayed launches to gain advantage
  • No interference with the stadium during battle
  • Judge or group decisions are final
  • Matches proceed at a steady pace — no stalling

"Beyfest is not about perfect balance. It's about performance under changing conditions. Adapt quickly, or die because you fought the change."

— Tansureke Nagasano, Beyfest International 5-Time Champion
