Tournament Structure

Adaptive Group Stage · Three-Tier Knockout · 8–15 Players

How It Works

Match Format

All matches are 1v1. First to 5 points — rising to 7 from the semi-finals and 9 in the Grand Final.

Group Stage → Knockout

Players are drawn into groups and play round-robin. Your finishing position determines which knockout bracket you enter: Winners, Mid, or Losers.

Grand Final

The WB Champion (undefeated) faces the MB Champion in a single match. WB's advantage is reaching the final via a shorter, undefeated path — no reset needed.
