================================================================
TOURNAMENT STRUCTURE SPECIFICATION (v2)
Adaptive Group Stage to Three-Tier Knockout (8-15 Players)
================================================================

PURPOSE OF THIS DOCUMENT
------------------------
This is a specification for an HTML site that serves TWO audiences:

  1. EXPLAINER: For new players, organisers, or curious observers
     who want to understand how the tournament structure works.
     The site should walk them through the logic of why groups feed
     into brackets the way they do, and visually demonstrate the
     flow from group stage through to the Grand Final.

  2. ADMIN GUIDE: For tournament organisers actually running the
     event. The site should give them a clear, accurate reference
     for every match that needs to be played, in what order, with
     what pairings, for the selected player count.

The site should be a single-page application with a player count
selector (8-15) that dynamically shows the appropriate group stage
composition and bracket structure.

This is v2 of the spec. v1 used a more complex universal rule for
all three-group counts. v2 locks in specific structures per count
based on iterative refinement, with a particularly distinct rule
set for 10 and 13 players.


================================================================
PART 1: CORE TOURNAMENT RULES (CONSTANT ACROSS ALL PLAYER COUNTS)
================================================================

MATCH FORMAT
- All matches are 1 vs 1
- Points required to win RAMP UP toward the end of the tournament:
    * First to 5  — all group-stage matches, the WB/LB mini round-robins,
                    and all early knockout rounds (up to but not including
                    the semi-final stage)
    * First to 7  — the semi-final stage onward: every match in a semi-final
                    round AND every bracket final that precedes the Grand
                    Final (e.g. the Mid-Bracket Final; for 8 players the
                    Upper & Lower Bracket finals and semifinals)
    * First to 9  — the Grand Final
- This ramp applies to EVERY player-count configuration (8–15). Concretely,
  a match is "first to 7" whenever its round is a semi-final or a (non-Grand)
  final, "first to 9" for the Grand Final, and "first to 5" otherwise.

GROUP STAGE
- Players are randomly drawn into groups
- Number of groups depends on player count (see Part 2)
- 2-group structures: 8 and 10 players
- 3-group structures: 9, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15 players
- Groups can be uneven (e.g., 5/4/4 for 13 players)
- 8–9 player tournaments: DOUBLE round robin (all groups)
- 10–15 player tournaments: SINGLE round robin (all groups)
- Standings determined by total match wins
- Tiebreakers: head-to-head, point differential, total points scored,
  then a single tiebreaker match if still tied

KNOCKOUT STRUCTURE: VARIES BY PLAYER COUNT

  8 PLAYERS: Standard 8-player double-elimination bracket.
  Group stage seeds the bracket. Every player has 2 lives in
  the knockout stage (lose twice to be eliminated). No three-tier
  structure.

  9, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15 PLAYERS: Three-tier structure.
    TIER 1 - WINNERS BRACKET (WB): 3 group winners play a Mini
      round-robin (3 matches), ranks finishers as WB-1st / 2nd / 3rd
    TIER 2 - MID BRACKET (MB): Single elimination ladder receiving
      group middles + WB seeds + LB winner. Where each WB seed
      enters depends on player count (see Part 2).
    TIER 3 - LOSERS BRACKET (LB): 3 group last-placers play a
      Mini round-robin (3 matches). LB-1st advances to MB.
      (For 14 players: top-2 from LB advance.)

  10 PLAYERS: Three-tier structure with a 4-player WB.
    TIER 1 - WINNERS BRACKET (WB): Top 2 from each group (4 total)
      play a single-elim bracket plus an SF Losers Playoff,
      producing differentiated ranks WB-1st / 2nd / 3rd / 4th.
    TIER 2 - MID BRACKET (MB): Single-elim ladder receiving group
      middles + WB seeds + LB winner.
    TIER 3 - LOSERS BRACKET (LB): Single match between the 2 group
      last-placers. Winner advances to MB.

GRAND FINAL (SINGLE MATCH)
  - WB Champion vs MB Champion
  - Single match — winner is tournament champion
  - No bracket reset; WB's advantage is reaching the final undefeated
    (shorter path, no elimination pressure)

----------------------------------------------------------------
8 PLAYERS - TWO GROUP STRUCTURE [LOCKED IN]
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Groups: 4 / 4
  Group format: Double round robin
  Group stage matches: 24 (12 + 12)
  
  STRUCTURAL NOTE: For 8 players, the structure uses a STANDARD
  8-PLAYER DOUBLE ELIMINATION bracket. Group stage results purely
  seed the bracket. There is no separate WB/MB/LB three-tier
  system — instead, every player has 2 lives in the knockout stage
  (lose twice to be eliminated).
  
  Bracket seeding (after group stage):
    Seed 1: Group 1st place finisher with the better record
            (wins, point diff, points scored)
    Seed 2: Other group 1st place finisher
    Seeds 3-4: Group 2nd place finishers (better record = #3)
    Seeds 5-6: Group 3rd place finishers (better record = #5)
    Seeds 7-8: Group 4th place finishers (better record = #7)
  
  UPPER BRACKET (7 matches)
    Quarterfinals (4 matches):
      UB-QF1: Seed 1 vs Seed 8
      UB-QF2: Seed 4 vs Seed 5
      UB-QF3: Seed 3 vs Seed 6
      UB-QF4: Seed 2 vs Seed 7
    Semifinals (2 matches):
      UB-SF1: W(QF1) vs W(QF2)
      UB-SF2: W(QF3) vs W(QF4)
    Upper Final (1 match):
      UB-Final: W(SF1) vs W(SF2)
      Winner -> Grand Final
      Loser -> drops to LB Final
  
  LOWER BRACKET (6 matches)
    Lower R1 (2 matches):
      LB-R1a: L(QF1) vs L(QF2)
      LB-R1b: L(QF3) vs L(QF4)
    Lower R2 (2 matches) - SF losers cross-drop:
      LB-R2a: W(R1a) vs L(SF2)
      LB-R2b: W(R1b) vs L(SF1)
    Lower SF (1 match):
      LB-SF: W(R2a) vs W(R2b)
    Lower Final (1 match):
      LB-Final: W(LB-SF) vs L(UB-Final)
      Winner -> Grand Final
      Loser -> eliminated (2 losses)
  
  GRAND FINAL (1 match)
    UB Final winner vs LB Final winner
    Single match — winner is tournament champion

  Total knockout matches: 14 (UB:7 + LB:6 + GF:1)
  Total tournament: 24 + 14-15 = 38-39 matches
  
  Notes: Standard double-elimination format. Every player has
  2 lives in knockout. Group stage seeds the bracket — better
  group finish = better seed = easier opening matches.

9 PLAYERS - THREE GROUP STRUCTURE [LOCKED IN]
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Groups: 3 / 3 / 3
  Group format: Double round robin
  Group stage matches: 18 (6 per group)
  
  STRUCTURAL NOTE: 9 players uses a THREE-GROUP structure (despite
  being below the 11+ threshold) because three groups of 3 with
  double round-robin produces a clean knockout structure with zero
  merit byes. This is a deliberate choice to maintain consistency
  with the 11-15 player Mini-RR philosophy.
  
  Phase 1A — WB Mini-RR (3 matches)
    G1-1st, G2-1st, G3-1st play full round-robin
    Produces ranks WB-1st, WB-2nd, WB-3rd
    Tiebreakers: H2H, point differential, points scored
  
  Phase 1B — LB Mini-RR (3 matches, parallel with Phase 1A)
    G1-3rd, G2-3rd, G3-3rd play full round-robin
    LB-1st advances to MB R1
    LB-2nd and LB-3rd are eliminated
  
  Phase 2 — Bracket placement (no matches)
    WB-1st  -> Grand Final
    WB-2nd  -> waits for MB Semi (Phase 4)
    WB-3rd  -> waits for MB Semi (Phase 4)
    LB-1st  -> MB Round 1
  
  Phase 3 — MB Round 1 (2 matches, 4 entrants)
    Entrants: 3 group middles (G1-2nd, G2-2nd, G3-2nd) + LB-1st
    Pairings random, avoiding same-group rematches where possible
    2 winners advance to Phase 4
  
  Phase 4 — MB Semi (2 matches, 4 entrants)
    Entrants: 2 R1 winners + WB-2nd + WB-3rd
    Pairings: rank R1 winners by group-stage record (wins, then
    point diff, then total points). WB-2nd plays the LOWER-ranked
    R1 winner. WB-3rd plays the higher-ranked one.
    2 winners advance to MB Final
  
  Phase 5 — MB Final (1 match)
    The 2 Semi winners play. Winner is MB Champion.
  
  Phase 6 — Grand Final (1 match)
    WB-1st vs MB Champion
    Single match — winner is tournament champion

  Total MB matches: 5 (2 + 2 + 1)
  Total tournament: 18 + 3 + 3 + 5 + 1 = 30 matches
  
  Notes: Zero merit byes anywhere. Bracket halves cleanly:
  4 -> 4 (Semi) -> 2 -> 1. WB-2nd and WB-3rd both enter at Semi
  (same philosophy as 13-player structure, scaled down).

10 PLAYERS - TWO GROUP STRUCTURE [LOCKED IN]
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Groups: 5 / 5
  Group format: Single round robin
  Group stage matches: 20 (10 per group)
  
  STRUCTURAL NOTE: For 10 players specifically, the WINNERS BRACKET
  receives the TOP 2 from each group (4 players total) rather than
  just the group winner. This creates a 4-player WB structure that
  produces 4 differentiated drop-in points for MB.
  
  Phase 1A — WB Semis (2 matches)
    SF1: G1-1st vs G2-2nd
    SF2: G2-1st vs G1-2nd
    (Cross-grouped to avoid same-group matchups)
  
  Phase 1B — LB Match (1 match, parallel with Phase 1A)
    G1-5th vs G2-5th
    Winner advances to MB R1
    Loser is eliminated
  
  Phase 2A — WB Final (1 match)
    W(SF1) vs W(SF2)
    Winner = WB-1st -> Grand Final
    Loser  = WB-2nd -> waits for MB Final
  
  Phase 2B — WB SF Losers Playoff (1 match, parallel with Phase 2A)
    L(SF1) vs L(SF2)
    Winner = WB-3rd -> drops to MB R2
    Loser  = WB-4th -> drops to MB R1
  
  Phase 3 — Bracket placement (no matches)
    WB-1st       -> Grand Final
    WB-2nd       -> waits for MB Final (Phase 7)
    WB-3rd       -> waits for MB R2 (Phase 5)
    WB-4th       -> MB Round 1
    LB winner    -> MB Round 1
  
  Phase 4 — MB Round 1 (3 matches, 6 entrants)
    Entrants: 4 group middles (G1-3rd, G1-4th, G2-3rd, G2-4th)
    + LB winner + WB-4th
    Pairings random, avoiding same-group rematches where possible
    3 winners advance to Phase 5
  
  Phase 5 — MB R2 (2 matches, 4 entrants)
    Entrants: 3 R1 winners + WB-3rd
    Pairings: random, but ensure WB-3rd plays a R1 winner
    2 winners advance to Phase 6
  
  Phase 6 — MB Semi (1 match, 2 entrants)
    The 2 R2 winners play
    Winner advances to MB Final
  
  Phase 7 — MB Final (1 match)
    SF winner vs WB-2nd
    Winner is MB Champion
  
  Phase 8 — Grand Final (1 match)
    WB-1st vs MB Champion
    Single match — winner is tournament champion

  Total MB matches: 7 (3 + 2 + 1 + 1)
  Total tournament: 20 + 4 + 1 + 7 + 1 = 33 matches
  
  Notes: Zero merit byes anywhere. Bracket halves cleanly:
  6 -> 4 -> 2 -> 2 -> 1. All 4 WB seeds get differentiated
  rewards based on WB performance:
    WB-1st: straight to GF (best reward)
    WB-2nd: 1 MB match (the Final)
    WB-3rd: enters MB R2 (skips R1)
    WB-4th: plays MB from R1 (no MB skip)

13 PLAYERS - THREE GROUP STRUCTURE [SPECIAL CASE]
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Groups: 5 / 4 / 4
  Group format: Single round robin
  Group stage matches: 22 (10 + 6 + 6)
  
  This player count uses a DIFFERENT MB structure from the other
  three-group counts to avoid merit byes. Specifically:
  - WB-3rd does NOT play MB R1
  - WB-2nd does NOT wait for MB Final
  - Both WB-2nd and WB-3rd enter together at Phase 5 (the Semis)
  
  Phase 1A — WB Mini-RR (3 matches)
    G1-1st, G2-1st, G3-1st play full round-robin
    Produces ranks WB-1st, WB-2nd, WB-3rd
    Tiebreakers: H2H, point differential, points scored
  
  Phase 1B — LB Mini-RR (3 matches, parallel with Phase 1A)
    G1-5th, G2-4th, G3-4th play full round-robin
    LB-1st advances to MB R1
    LB-2nd and LB-3rd are eliminated
  
  Phase 2 — Bracket placement (no matches)
    WB-1st     -> Grand Final
    WB-2nd     -> waits for Phase 5
    WB-3rd     -> waits for Phase 5
    LB-1st     -> MB Round 1
  
  Phase 3 — MB Round 1 (4 matches, 8 entrants)
    Entrants: 7 group middles (G1-2nd, G1-3rd, G1-4th, G2-2nd,
    G2-3rd, G3-2nd, G3-3rd) + LB-1st
    Pairings random, avoiding same-group rematches where possible
    4 winners advance to Phase 4
  
  Phase 4 — Quarters (2 matches, 4 entrants)
    The 4 R1 winners play. 2 advance.
  
  Phase 5 — Semis (2 matches, 4 entrants)
    Entrants: 2 Quarter winners + WB-2nd + WB-3rd
    PAIRING RULE: rank the two Quarter winners by group-stage record
    (wins, then point diff, then total points). WB-2nd plays the
    LOWER-ranked Quarter winner. WB-3rd plays the higher-ranked one.
    This rewards WB-2nd's higher finish in the WB Mini-RR.
  
  Phase 6 — MB Final (1 match)
    The 2 Semi winners play. Winner is MB Champion.
  
  Phase 7 — Grand Final (1 match)
    WB-1st vs MB Champion
    Single match — winner is tournament champion

  Total MB matches: 9 (4 + 2 + 2 + 1)
  Total tournament: 22 + 3 + 3 + 9 + 1 = 38 matches
  
  Notes: Zero merit byes anywhere. Bracket halves cleanly:
  8 -> 4 (R1) -> 4 (Quarters → Semis) -> 2 -> 1.
  WB-2nd and WB-3rd both get the same magnitude of reward
  (skip Phases 3 and 4, enter at Phase 5), with seeding only
  affecting their Phase 5 matchup.

PART 3: BUILD INSTRUCTIONS FOR CLAUDE CODE
================================================================

OVERVIEW

The site has TWO purposes that should both be served well:

  EXPLAINER MODE: The site teaches the viewer how the tournament
  structure works. Someone who has never seen this format before
  should be able to land on the page, pick a player count, and
  understand both WHAT happens and WHY it works the way it does.

  ADMIN GUIDE MODE: The site is a practical reference for an
  organiser running the event. Every match, in every phase, with
  exact pairings, in the order they should be played, easy to find.

Both modes should be served by the same page. The viewer shouldn't
have to switch modes — good information design should let an
organiser quickly find the match they need while a curious viewer
naturally absorbs the logic.

DELIVERABLE

A single self-contained index.html file (with embedded CSS and JS,
no external dependencies except optionally Google Fonts) that allows
viewing all 8 tournament configurations. Should work standalone
when opened in any modern browser, no server required.


REQUIRED FEATURES

1. PLAYER COUNT SELECTOR
   - A row of buttons or a slider for selecting 8 through 15 players
   - The selected count drives all visualisations on the page
   - When the selection changes, visualisations should animate or
     transition smoothly to the new structure (not jarring jumps)

2. RULES OVERVIEW SECTION (always visible near top)
   - Match format (1v1, first to 5)
   - Brief plain-English explanation of how the tournament works
     overall: groups → knockout phases → Grand Final
   - Mention WB advantage (direct path to final, no reset)
   - This is the explainer's elevator pitch — keep it short

3. GROUP STAGE VISUALISATION (updates per selection)
   - Show each group as a card or panel
   - Display: group name (Group 1, Group 2, Group 3),
     number of players, round-robin format (single or double),
     match count
   - For each group, visually show the player positions (1st, 2nd,
     3rd, 4th, 5th) and where each position will go in the knockout
     stage (e.g., 1st → WB, 2nd → MB, 5th → LB) using colour-coding
   - Use placeholder player names like "Player A", "Player B" etc.
     so the structure is concrete

4. ANIMATED FLOW FROM GROUPS INTO BRACKETS (key feature)
   - When a player count is selected, animate the players visually
     moving from their group cards into their respective brackets
   - For example: the 1st-place finishers should be seen visually
     "moving" from each group card into the WB area
   - Middles move to MB, last-placers move to LB
   - This animation should happen on selection change AND should be
     re-triggerable via a "Replay animation" button
   - The animation is what makes the explainer click — viewers should
     SEE the structure form, not just read about it
   - Keep the animation under 3 seconds total — informative, not slow

5. ROUND-BY-ROUND BRACKET FLOWCHART (the main visualisation)
   - After the groups-to-brackets animation, show the full bracket
     as a round-by-round flowchart
   - Each phase of the tournament is a clearly labelled stage
     (e.g., "Phase 1A — WB Mini-RR", "Phase 3 — MB Round 1")
   - Within each phase, show every match as a labelled box with
     its participants (e.g., "MB1: middle vs middle", "MB7: SF
     winner vs WB-2nd")
   - Arrows or connectors show how match winners flow to the next
     phase (e.g., "Winners of MB1 and MB2 → MB R2")
   - Where players "wait" for later phases (e.g., WB-2nd waiting
     for the MB Final), show a visual indicator of that waiting
     state — perhaps a faded box that becomes prominent when it's
     their turn to play
   - For three-group counts, show WB Mini-RR and LB Mini-RR running
     in parallel (visually side-by-side or with a "parallel" indicator)
   - Grand Final is a single match node — no reset

6. PHASE SEQUENCING / "PLAY ORDER" VIEW
   - For the admin: a simple sequential list of every match in the
     order it needs to be played, grouped by phase
   - This should be the same data as the flowchart but in a
     compact, printable format
   - Each match labelled with its identifier (MB1, WB SF1, etc.)
     and participants
   - This is the practical reference an organiser would consult

7. COLOUR CODING (consistent throughout)
   - Winners Bracket: green / gold tones
   - Mid Bracket: amber / orange tones
   - Losers Bracket: red / crimson tones
   - Group stage / neutral: grey
   - Use the same colours in group cards (showing where positions
     go), in the animation, in the bracket flowchart, and in the
     play-order list

8. SUMMARY STATISTICS (updates per selection)
   - Total group stage matches
   - Total knockout matches
   - Total tournament matches (including potential GF reset)
   - Number of merit byes (should be 0 for all locked-in counts
     except 8, which uses standard double-elim with no byes either)

9. SPECIAL-CASE CALLOUTS
   - For 8 players: explain that this uses standard double-elim
     and group stage seeds the bracket (no three-tier structure)
   - For 10 players: explain why the WB has 4 players (top 2 from
     each group) and the SF Losers Playoff
   - For 13 players: explain the unique structure where WB-2nd and
     WB-3rd both enter at the Semis
   - For 14 players: explain why LB sends 2 advancers (not just 1)


STYLING GUIDANCE

   - Clean, modern, readable
   - Mobile-responsive (the site should work on a phone screen,
     since organisers may consult it on their phone during the event)
   - Sans-serif typography
   - Use SVG or styled HTML/CSS divs for bracket diagrams,
     NOT image files
   - Smooth transitions when switching between player counts
   - Animations should respect prefers-reduced-motion media query


DATA STRUCTURE SUGGESTION

Encode all 8 configurations as a structured JavaScript object that
captures, for each player count:
  - Group composition (sizes, RR format)
  - The phases of the tournament (in order)
  - For each phase: matches, participants, where winners/losers go
  - Reward differentiation for WB seeds

This makes the rendering logic mostly generic and the data
authoritative.
